# Full Milestone Update Sweep — 20 August 2026

## Scope and method

This review covered all 50 public project cards. The PCDS 2030 framework overview was checked as context but excluded from the project count. Discovery began with each exact project name plus `project milestones` and current-status or 2026 update variants, split into five ten-card batches. The parent review then repeated the live sweep and opened the underlying pages for every accepted claim.

Search snippets, generated summaries and broad roundups were used only to find leads. A claim was accepted only after the underlying page supported the same project identity, tracked scope, date and delivery event. Google eventually returned an unusual-traffic interstitial, so the remaining focused searches used Bing and direct official or publisher pages. No target was converted into a completed milestone without confirmation.

1. **Bau Gold Project:** Besra's 31 July 2026 disclosure confirms formal acceptance of the conditional renewal offer for Mining Lease ML 05/2012/1D. A completed milestone was added; final lease conditions remain open.
2. **Sarawak Bioindustrial Park:** Sarawak Biodiversity Centre confirms that Central Hub construction commenced on 13 August 2026 and describes a 24-month construction period. A completed construction-start milestone and an August 2028 Central Hub completion target were added. The source does not apply that 24-month target to the separate SBC Bioprocess Commercial Centre.

4. **Sarawak Infectious Disease Centre:** an opened 10 August 2026 report says the campus is nearing completion. The page was added as supporting evidence, but no milestone was added or completed because the wording is not a distinct delivery event. The October 2026 completion milestone remains open.

A follow-up review of the displayed Next Milestone for every card confirmed that the Special Needs Community Centre is the only card whose next outcome changes in this release: completing the tender milestone advances it to the November 2026 letter of award. Bau retains finalisation of its lease conditions, SIDC retains campus completion, and the Bioindustrial Park retains anchor and research partner selection because BioVerde's live project page says that selection is currently under way.
