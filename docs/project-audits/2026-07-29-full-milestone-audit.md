# Full Tracker Milestone Audit

Audit date: 29 July 2026

## Purpose

This audit reviews all 35 public tracker records, comprising 34 project cards and the overarching
PCDS 2030 framework, against the milestone-writing and evidence rules in
`docs/data-methodology.md`. It focuses on whether each milestone is a meaningful public event or
delivery outcome, whether open targets remain honest, and whether the sequence gives readers a
useful view of the project lifecycle.

The audit does not require every card to have the same number of milestones. A short sequence is
preferred when public evidence does not support more detail. Future milestones use `TBD` only when
a public source identifies the deliverable but does not publish a reliable date.

## Evidence Method

- Existing card sources and the repository's dated source reviews were checked first.
- The official UKAS page for Baram and the MEESty and The Star pages for Batang Ai were opened
  again on 29 July 2026.
- Completed milestones require an explicit completed, commissioned, launched, operational,
  approved, signed, awarded, or equivalent claim.
- Passed targets without completion evidence were changed to `TBD` or retained as open.
- Context-only announcements, generic activity, and ordinary ongoing operations were removed
  where they did not help readers understand delivery.
- No standard project stages were invented to make a card look more complete.

## Lifecycle Completeness Gate

Every card was also checked against its intended public outcome:

- A `Planning` or `In Progress` card must retain at least one open milestone.
- An announcement, approval, agreement, design stage, or contract award cannot make a capital
  project appear complete when construction, commissioning, operations, production, or another
  supported delivery outcome remains.
- `Operational`, `Designated`, and `Enacted` cards must not retain open delivery milestones.
- Completed milestones use definitive past-tense outcomes. Open milestones use active
  present-tense outcomes or an unambiguous pending-event noun phrase.
- A lifecycle stage is included only when a public source identifies it. Missing standard stages
  are not inferred solely to make every card follow the same template.

The 35-record review found no non-completed project card with every milestone marked complete.
Ambiguous open-milestone wording was corrected across the affected cards, and the automated
content validator now enforces the structural rules above.

## Implemented Site-Wide Wording Corrections

Open milestone wording was standardised without changing the supporting dates or completion flags
for SMD Semiconductor, the paddy programme, Semenggoh, Piasau, Bau Gold, the Sarawak Cancer
Centre, Sarawak Bioindustrial Park, Kota Petra, the international-school expansion, Pan Borneo
Highway, the Sarawak-Sabah Link Road, the Sarawak-Singapore interconnection, the Sarawak River
system, KUTS, the new Kuching airport, Baleh, Batang Ai, and Baram.

Examples:

- `500,000-tonne annual rice output achieved` became
  `Annual rice output reaches 500,000 tonnes`.
- `Federal airport development approval obtained` became
  `Federal authorities approve Airport Development Request`.
- `Pilot operations begin` became `ART pilot service begins`.
- `Project completion` became `Cancer centre reaches completion`.
- `Supporting township is completed` became
  `Supporting township reaches completion`.

## Changed-Card Evidence

### Niah Caves Archaeological Heritage Site

- [UNESCO World Heritage Centre](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1014) gives the official listed
  property name as `The Archaeological Heritage of Niah National Park’s Caves Complex`.
- UNESCO identifies Sarawak Forestry Corporation and the Sarawak Museum Department as the main
  responsible government institutions, with Sarawak Forestry Corporation taking the management
  lead.
- The canonical project identity and lead field were aligned to the official page. The shorter
  display name remains scan-friendly.

### PCDS 2030 Overarching Framework

- [Business Events Sarawak](https://businesseventssarawak.com/about-sarawak/pcds2030/)
  remains the live public framework and target source.
- [DayakDaily, 7 July 2026](https://dayakdaily.com/world-bank-sarawak-retains-high-income-state-status-with-gni-per-capita-of-rm70500-in-2025/)
  supports the completed high-income milestone.
- The Sarawak Government strategy PDF remains research context but was removed from the live-card
  source list.

### Baram Agrovoltaic Project

- [UKAS, 29 March 2026](https://ukas.sarawak.gov.my/web/subpage/news_view/37208)
  names the project and identifies the modern agriculture estate, paddy component, logistics hub,
  300MW power station, and supporting township. It does not publish construction or operating dates.
- The four component outcomes therefore remain `done: false` with `TBD` dates.
- The 2024 Planet QEOS proposal and the 2025 Baram DeepTech Energy Programme announcements describe
  different capacities, land areas, investments, and partner scopes. They are not accepted as proof
  that those parties implement the exact RM6 billion project.
- The lead remains `Not disclosed`, and the separate programme's agreement milestone and sources
  were removed from the live card.

### Batang Ai Floating Solar Farm

- [The Star, 6 October 2025](https://www.thestar.com.my/news/nation/2025/10/06/sarawak-expands-solar-capacity-in-push-for-renewable-energy-says-abang-johari)
  explicitly reports that the 50MW floating solar farm was commissioned in December 2024 and that
  Sarawak Energy would add 120MW.
- [MEESty, 19 August 2025](https://meesty.sarawak.gov.my/web/subpage/news_view/54)
  records the earlier December operations target.
- The 50MW commissioning is complete. The 120MW addition remains open because neither source
  confirms that the expansion has been delivered.

### Sarawak Bioindustrial Park

- The PCDS 2030 Highlights 2023 report remains research context only. Page 88 gives Q3 2027 for
  Phase 1 construction and 2035 for full operations, while page 89 separately refers to Phase 1
  completion in 2026. No current project-specific webpage independently confirms those dates, so
  they are not shown on the live card.
- [BioVerde](https://www.bioverde.com.my/sbp) describes the park as under development, says anchor
  and research partners are being selected, and identifies pilot bioprocessing, analytical
  laboratory, and GMP manufacturing facilities.
- [BioVerde's company page](https://www.bioverde.com.my/about) identifies park development and
  operations as part of its mandate.
- The card therefore remains `Planning`. Partner selection, facility completion, and park
  operations remain open `TBD` outcomes with `done: false`.

### H2biscus and H2ornbill Hydrogen Projects

- [Samsung E&A, 24 November 2023](https://www.samsungena.com/en/newsroom/news/view?idx=15543)
  confirms the September 2022 H2biscus renewable-power memorandum and the 23 November 2023
  commencement of front-end engineering design.
- [Borneo Post, 19 December 2023](https://www.theborneopost.com/2023/12/19/sedc-energy-sumitomo-and-eneos-fine-tuning-project-h2ornbill-details/)
  confirms the October 2023 H2ornbill joint development agreement among SEDC Energy, ENEOS,
  and Sumitomo.
- [MIDA, 22 May 2024](https://www.mida.gov.my/mida-news/sarawaks-new-energy-hub-to-receive-rm2-16-trillion-investment-by-2050-says-abang-johari/)
  confirms the H2biscus joint development agreement and the project's FEED scope.
- [MEESty, source-news date 9 June 2025](https://meesty.sarawak.gov.my/web/subpage/news_view/40)
  records that both projects had reached FEED. The page's older production and FEED-completion
  targets are not retained after later scope reductions.
- [Free Malaysia Today, 8 April 2026](https://www.freemalaysiatoday.com/category/nation/2026/04/08/sarawak-hydrogen-projects-scaled-down-over-weak-demand/)
  reports that both projects reduced planned production capacity because of weak demand and
  uncertain offtake.
- [Borneo Post, 27 May 2026](https://www.theborneopost.com/2026/05/27/sarawaks-hydrogen-initiative-scaled-down-on-funding-constraints/)
  quotes the SEDC Energy chairman saying that the Japanese partners suspended H2ornbill's original
  Japan-export concept while considering other hydrogen carriers.
- [Borneo Post, 9 June 2026](https://www.theborneopost.com/2026/06/09/sarawak-recalibrates-h2biscus-and-h2ornbill-hydrogen-projects-amid-financial-constraints/)
  records the state government's scope recalibration and near-term financial constraints.
- The card remains `Planning`. All seven evidenced development events are complete. The updated
  project plan and production timeline, final investment decisions, construction start, and
  commercial production remain open and undated.
- The lead is narrowed to SEDC Energy. Gentari is a partner in the hub's common facilities, not
  the verified lead for both export projects.
- The reported value is changed to `Not disclosed`. The February 2024 US$4.2 billion report
  covered the export projects together with wider hub and Rembus facilities and predates the
  later capacity reductions.

## Residual Risks

- Public confirmation is still missing for SMD's IP registration, Sungai Baji operations and
  production, Semenggoh Phase 1 completion, Pan Borneo Work Package 11, and the Batang Ai 120MW
  expansion.
- Baram's components are identifiable, but the delivery order, contracts, dates, and party
  responsibility across the broader RM6 billion scope remain unclear.
- H2biscus and H2ornbill have no published post-recalibration project plan, value, FEED outcome,
  final investment decision, construction start, or commercial production timeline.
- SCORE is a broad development programme. Its current open milestones should be revisited when
  measurable, time-bound delivery evidence is published.
