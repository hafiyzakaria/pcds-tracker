# Full Tracker Milestone Audit

Audit date: 29 July 2026

## Purpose

This audit reviews all 35 public tracker records, comprising 34 project cards and the overarching
PCDS 2030 framework, against the milestone-writing and evidence rules in
`docs/data-methodology.md`. It focuses on whether each milestone is a meaningful public event or
delivery outcome, whether open targets remain honest, and whether the sequence gives readers a
useful view of the project lifecycle.

The audit does not require every card to have the same number of milestones. A short sequence is
preferred when public evidence does not support more detail. Future milestones use `TBD` only when
a public source identifies the deliverable but does not publish a reliable date.

## Evidence Method

- Existing card sources and the repository's dated source reviews were checked first.
- The official UKAS page for Baram and the MEESty and The Star pages for Batang Ai were opened
  again on 29 July 2026.
- Completed milestones require an explicit completed, commissioned, launched, operational,
  approved, signed, awarded, or equivalent claim.
- Passed targets without completion evidence were changed to `TBD` or retained as open.
- Context-only announcements, generic activity, and ordinary ongoing operations were removed
  where they did not help readers understand delivery.
- No standard project stages were invented to make a card look more complete.

## Lifecycle Completeness Gate

Every card was also checked against its intended public outcome:

- A `Planning` or `In Progress` card must retain at least one open milestone.
- An announcement, approval, agreement, design stage, or contract award cannot make a capital
  project appear complete when construction, commissioning, operations, production, or another
  supported delivery outcome remains.
- `Operational`, `Designated`, and `Enacted` cards must not retain open delivery milestones.
- Completed milestones use definitive past-tense outcomes. Open milestones use active
  present-tense outcomes or an unambiguous pending-event noun phrase.
- A lifecycle stage is included only when a public source identifies it. Missing standard stages
  are not inferred solely to make every card follow the same template.

The 35-record review found no non-completed project card with every milestone marked complete.
Ambiguous open-milestone wording was corrected across the affected cards, and the automated
content validator now enforces the structural rules above.

## Implemented Site-Wide Wording Corrections

Open milestone wording was standardised without changing the supporting dates or completion flags
for SMD Semiconductor, the paddy programme, Semenggoh, Piasau, Bau Gold, the Sarawak Cancer
Centre, Sarawak Bioindustrial Park, Kota Petra, the international-school expansion, Pan Borneo
Highway, the Sarawak-Sabah Link Road, the Sarawak-Singapore interconnection, the Sarawak River
system, KUTS, the new Kuching airport, Baleh, Batang Ai, and Baram.

Examples:

- `500,000-tonne annual rice output achieved` became
  `Annual rice output reaches 500,000 tonnes`.
- `Federal airport development approval obtained` became
  `Federal authorities approve Airport Development Request`.
- `Pilot operations begin` became `ART pilot service begins`.
- `Project completion` became `Cancer centre reaches completion`.
- `Supporting township is completed` became
  `Supporting township reaches completion`.

### Tanjung Embang Deep-Sea Port follow-up

- Review date: 29 July 2026
- Queries included the exact project name with `value`, `cost`, `investment`, `tender`,

- The InvestSarawak page carrying the same paragraph is retained as research context only. Its main
  subject and title concern the Bintulu Port takeover, so it is not used as a live-card source.

  retains the Sarawak Government and PETROS partnership and the 2032 operations target.
- [The Edge Malaysia](https://theedgemalaysia.com/node/734490) reported on 25 November 2024
  that the Kuching deep-sea port with LNG facilities was estimated at RM25 billion to RM30 billion.
  [CIDB Malaysia](https://www.cidb.gov.my/eng/sarawak-embarking-two-mega-projects-at-once/)
  connected the same estimate specifically to the Tanjung Embang deep-sea port and gas terminal
  on 27 January 2025. The card therefore uses `Estimated RM25-30 billion`.
- The RM100 billion figure combines the port and airport, while RM300 billion refers to the wider
  Sarawak Gas Roadmap. Neither is used as the port's reported value.
- Status remains `Planning`. No accepted source confirms procurement, construction commencement,
  commissioning, or operations.
- Outcome: update the summary, milestones, sources, and BM rendering. No new update-history entry is
  needed because the audit clarifies earlier lifecycle evidence rather than recording a newer
  material event.
