## Lubok Punggor AgriHub and Mid Sadong 1 Irrigation Project

### Identity and scope

The tracked unit is the RM30 million initial irrigation project at Lubok Punggor and the associated AgriHub implementation scope. It is distinct from the statewide RM1 Billion Paddy Infrastructure Programme because the live sources identify its own location, 54.4-hectare initial scope, approval, launch, compensation and physical components.

The canonical name retains both public identities: the Department of Irrigation and Drainage article calls the launched work the Mid Sadong 1 Rehabilitation Scheme, while UKAS calls the wider implementation the Lubok Punggor AgriHub development project. The card does not present the wider RM1 billion programme or a proposed 500-hectare expansion as this project's value.

### Accepted evidence

- [DID Sarawak, 29 November 2024](https://did.sarawak.gov.my/web/subpage/news_view/821) confirms Sarawak Government delivery through DID Sarawak, the RM30 million project, February 2024 approval, 54.4 hectares of paddy fields and the Mid Sadong 1 launch.
- [UKAS, 9 March 2026](https://ukas.sarawak.gov.my/web/subpage/news_view/35719) confirms the 8 March handover of RM6.5 million in compensation to 119 recipients and identifies the AgriHub components: a warehouse and machinery workshop, water reservoir, and irrigation and drainage construction or upgrading.

### Field decisions

- Status: `In Progress`, based on the achieved approval, launch and compensation stages with delivery works still open.
- Value: `RM30 million`, accepted as the project-specific amount for the initial 54.4-hectare phase. The RM6.5 million compensation is a component payment, not the project value. The RM1 billion figure is the statewide programme allocation and the approximate RM100 million figure concerns a possible 500-hectare expansion, so both are excluded.
- Milestones: approval, Mid Sadong 1 launch and compensation are complete; AgriHub and irrigation completion remains open and undated.
- Update history: add the 8 March 2026 compensation and implementation-scope event because it is a meaningful public delivery step.

## Bako National Park ASEAN Heritage Park

### Identity and scope

The tracked unit is Bako National Park's ASEAN Heritage Park designation, not a construction project or the wider Bako-Buntal Bay conservation landscape. It is separate from Lambir Hills because ASEAN assigned each park its own numbered designation and describes each park independently.

### Accepted evidence

- [DayakDaily, 23 August 2025](https://dayakdaily.com/sarawak-nominates-bako-bukit-lambir-as-asean-heritage-parks-to-elevate-global-conservation-status/) confirms the Sarawak Government nomination and the ASEAN Centre for Biodiversity evaluation conducted from 6 to 13 May 2025.
- [ASEAN Centre for Biodiversity, 22 September 2025](https://www.aseanbiodiversity.org/news-events-jobs/six-natural-wonders-in-malaysia-and-viet-nam-declared-as-asean-heritage-parks/) confirms Bako as the 65th ASEAN Heritage Park, its 2,727-hectare scope and its conservation characteristics.
- [BIMP-EAGA, 9 January 2026](https://bimp-eaga.asia/articles/bimp-eaga-now-home-16-asean-heritage-parks) independently confirms that Bako entered the network during the September 2025 ASEAN ministerial meeting.

### Field decisions

- Status: `Designated`.
- Lead: `Sarawak Government`. The ASEAN Centre for Biodiversity is the programme secretariat and evaluation body, not the park's delivery lead.
- Value: `Not disclosed`. The designation is not assigned a public project cost. Exact-name, location, designation, government, official-domain, cost, value, allocation, investment, funding, `kos`, `nilai projek`, `peruntukan`, `pelaburan`, `juta` and `bilion` variants found no defensible project-specific monetary amount.
- Milestones: May 2025 evaluation, August 2025 public nomination confirmation and September 2025 designation are complete.
- Update history: add the 22 September 2025 ASEAN Centre for Biodiversity confirmation as the authoritative designation record.

## Sarawak High Performance Centre

### Identity and scope

The tracked unit is the planned high-tech sports-science and elite-athlete development centre within the Sarawak Sports Complex in Petra Jaya. The 13th Malaysia Plan executive summary names the `Sarawak High Performance Sports Centre` as a professional sports-development facility, while the latest UKAS reporting uses `Sarawak High Performance Centre`. The card follows the newer public name and does not treat proposed divisional centres in older reporting as part of this facility.

### Accepted evidence

- [UKAS, 18 February 2026](https://ukas.sarawak.gov.my/web/subpage/news_view/34514) confirms that the Sarawak Sports Complex site was identified, describes the sports-science purpose and 67 to 68-acre wider complex, and records the intended Sarawak Government, federal ministry and National Sports Institute collaboration.
- [UKAS, 13 June 2026](https://ukas.sarawak.gov.my/web/subpage/news_view/42510) supersedes the older 2025 operation expectation. It places the centre in planning and study, identifies MYSED as the state delivery ministry, states that documentation and site work are beginning, and says construction is expected after the 2027 SEA Games.

### Field decisions

- Status: `Planning`. A site and project concept exist, but no physical construction start is reported.
- Lead: `Sarawak Government / Ministry of Youth, Sports and Entrepreneur Development`. The National Sports Institute is a planned sports-science support partner, not the principal delivery body.
- Value: `Not disclosed`. Exact-name, alias, site, ministry, English and BM searches for cost, value, allocation, investment, funding, `kos`, `nilai projek`, `anggaran`, `peruntukan`, `pelaburan`, `juta` and `bilion` found no project-specific amount. UKAS says the project will be state funded with National Sports Institute cooperation but gives no figure.
- Milestones: the February 2026 site and concept announcement and June 2026 planning-stage confirmation are complete; post-SEA Games construction and eventual operations remain open.
- Update history: add the 13 June 2026 planning-stage and construction-sequence update because it resolves the older schedule conflict.

## FR21 Jalan Serian-Tebedu-Indonesia Border Upgrade

### Identity and scope

The tracked unit is the proposed upgrade of Federal Route 21 between Serian, Tebedu and the Indonesian border from two lanes to four lanes. JKR Sarawak's August 2024 project briefing defines the official preliminary-design scope as 42 km. The November 2025 parliamentary report describes the project as about 48 km and includes 10 new bridges. The card anchors the project to JKR's 42 km official scope and retains the wider 48 km and bridge description as explicitly qualified reporting rather than silently merging the figures.

### Accepted evidence

- [JKR Sarawak, 2 August 2024](https://jkr.sarawak.gov.my/web/subpage/news_view/614) confirms the preliminary works and design briefing for `Projek Naik Taraf FR21 Jalan Serian-Tebedu-Sempadan Indonesia` and describes the 42 km, two-lane to four-lane scope.
- [NADMA republication of TVS parliamentary reporting, 6 November 2025](https://www.nadma.gov.my/bm/media-2/berita/5439-dewan-rakyat-projek-naik-taraf-jalan-serian-tebedu-dijangka-cecah-rm1-bilion) records the Deputy Works Minister's statement that the reported 48 km project and 10 new bridges could cost more than RM500 million and possibly reach RM1 billion. It says the actual estimate will be finalised with the Ministry of Economy after preliminary work targeted for the end of 2026.
- JKR Sarawak's 2026 consultancy retender remains procurement context rather than a live-card source because the public result is a tender attachment. It confirms continuing preliminary engineering, road-safety, traffic, electrical and related consultancy procurement.

### Value and scope decisions

- Status: `Planning`. Preliminary design, studies and consultancy procurement do not prove a physical construction start.
- Lead: `Federal Ministry of Works / JKR Sarawak`, reflecting the federal-route owner and Sarawak delivery agency.
- Value: `RM0.5-1 billion`. The summary preserves the source meaning: the preliminary estimate is more than RM500 million and may reach RM1 billion, not a final approved cost.
- Rejected value: [myBina project 97982](https://www.mybina.com.my/project/97982-fr21-jalan-serian-tebedu-sempadan-indonesia-road-upgrading-work-consultancy-services) lists `Between 10 and 50 million`, but the page is explicitly scoped to consultancy services. It is not the road upgrade's total value.
- Rejected older value: the RM150 million Budget 2023 allocation is older and does not match the later four-lane, bridge-inclusive preliminary scope. It is not displayed as the current project estimate.
- Milestones: the 2024 stakeholder briefing and 2025 parliamentary disclosure are complete; preliminary-work completion and cost finalisation remain open for late 2026, while construction remains open and undated.
- Update history: add the 5 November 2025 parliamentary estimate because it materially defines the provisional value and next decision point.

## Bintulu Bio-Algae Initial Commercial Plant

### Identity and role resolution

The tracked unit is the Bintulu Bio-Algae Initial Commercial Plant named in the PCDS 2030 Highlights 2023, where the image caption also calls it an `Initial Commercial Pilot (ICP) plant`. It is not CHITOSE Carbon Capture Central Sarawak at Sejingkat. The Bintulu project cultivates and harvests microalgae and extracts crude algae oil for later SAF refining, while C4 Sarawak uses Sejingkat flue gas for a separately named carbon-capture and biomass facility.

The identity blocker is resolved by the source chain:

- [PETRONAS, 21 July 2022](https://www.petronas.com/bm/node/40) explicitly says PETRONAS Research Sdn Bhd and SEDC Energy will explore an `Initial Commercial Plant (ICP)` for microalgae cultivation, harvesting and crude algae oil extraction, with a later large-scale commercial plant subject to commercial attractiveness.
- [PETRONAS, 26 January 2023](https://www.petronas.com/media/media-releases/petronas-sedc-energy-develop-microalgae-oil-sustainable-aviation-fuel) records a signed agreement under which PETRONAS Research and SEDC Energy jointly develop cultivation, harvesting and extraction technology for crude algae oil that would later be refined into SAF.
- [DayakDaily, 16 September 2024](https://dayakdaily.com/premier-swak-to-build-bio-refinery-in-bintulu-once-microalgae-cultivation-on-10000-acre-site-starts/) confirms that studies were under way in Bintulu by SEDC Energy together with PETRONAS, with a planned 10,000-acre expansion and a later Bintulu bio-refinery after cultivation scale-up.
- A July 2024 SEDC Energy RFQ discovered through its official social account uses the exact name `Bio Algae Initial Commercial Plant (ICP) Project at Bintulu Sarawak` and reference `PDT-RFQ-24-010`. The Instagram body was not publicly readable in the review browser, so it remains discovery and corroboration context rather than a live-card source.

### Field decisions

- Status: `In Progress`, based on the signed joint-development arrangements and the 2024 confirmation that Bintulu studies were under way. This does not imply that the 10,000-acre commercial scale-up or bio-refinery has begun.
- Lead: `SEDC Energy / PETRONAS Research`. Both are evidenced joint technology-development parties. The Sarawak Premier is an announcing policy leader, not assigned delivery control, and the later bio-refinery is a dependent future facility rather than the current card lead.
- Value: `Not disclosed`. Exact-name, `ICP`, Bintulu, PRSB, SEDC Energy, microalgae, SAF, English and BM value searches found no project-specific cost, allocation, investment, contract or financing amount. The RM22.8 billion figure surfaced beside the project in a PCDS search result is an aggregate approved-investment figure and is rejected.
- Milestones: the July 2022 ICP collaboration, January 2023 technology-development agreement and September 2024 Bintulu study and scale-up confirmation are complete. The 10,000-acre scale-up remains open to 2030.
- Update history: add the 16 September 2024 Bintulu studies and scale-up announcement because it is the latest verified public development.
- Recommendation: add now. The prior identity and role blocker is resolved at medium-high confidence by an explicit PETRONAS ICP source, a second PETRONAS joint-development source and current Bintulu reporting. Re-review when SEDC Energy or PETRONAS publishes an accessible award, final investment decision, commercial-scale construction start or project-specific value.

## Inclusion-gate result

- Distinct tracked units: passed. Lubok Punggor has a project-specific initial phase; Bako and Lambir Hills have separately numbered ASEAN designations; the sports centre and federal-road upgrade have named facility and route scopes; the Bintulu ICP is distinct from C4 Sarawak and from its possible later bio-refinery.
- PCDS connection: passed. The PCDS 2030 Highlights 2023 identifies Lubok Punggor and the Bintulu ICP, the PCDS AIP Volume II Forestry material identifies the Bako and Lambir Hills designation initiative, and the 13th Malaysia Plan executive summary identifies the sports centre and federal-route upgrade.
- Exact identity, location, lead and scope: passed at the conservative levels recorded above.
- Live public evidence: passed. Every displayed field is supported by the opened webpages listed above; the PCDS PDFs are retained only as the inclusion basis.
- Status, milestone and completion claims: passed. Targets are not marked complete without achieved-event evidence.
- Value treatment: passed. Lubok Punggor uses the scope-matched RM30 million amount; the road uses the source-qualified preliminary RM0.5-1 billion range; the two designation cards, sports centre and Bio-Algae ICP retain `Not disclosed` after value exhaustion.
- English and Bahasa Melayu parity: required and implemented for all six cards, their milestones and public update entries.
