## Research method

Every candidate received the mandatory exact-name `project milestones` discovery pass, ordinary-result review, current-year status search, field-specific searches, value exhaustion, and a counter-search for delays, cancellation, completion, revised scope, and identity collisions. Generated summaries and result snippets were used for discovery only. Every source accepted for a live card was opened and checked against the visible page.

Google and later Bing presented search challenges during the audit. No challenge was solved or bypassed. Direct verification continued for URLs already surfaced, and no card relies on a snippet or generated search summary.

## Accepted evidence

### Rambungan Sustainable Shrimp Aquaculture Project

- [M-FICORD, 8 January 2025](https://mficord.sarawak.gov.my/web/subpage/news_view/1513) confirms the harvest programme, Loba Stoh location, 297-hectare land permission, Rambungan Aqua Life Sdn Bhd, and its joint-venture parties.
- [DayakDaily, 8 January 2025](https://dayakdaily.com/rambungan-large-scale-shrimp-farming-targets-1349-tonnes-rm27-mln-in-annual-exports/) supplies the public project name, planned 94-pond expansion, and 2031 production and annual-export targets.
- `RM27 million` is annual export impact, not project cost. It is retained only as an open project-output milestone.
- M-FICORD alternates between `Rambungan Aqua Life` and `Rambungan Aquatic`. The canonical card uses `Rambungan Aqua Life Sdn Bhd`, which the same official page uses for the joint-venture record.

### Marudi Forest Conservation and Restoration Project

- [The Edge Malaysia, 16 March 2023](https://theedgemalaysia.com/node/659392) confirms the Marudi forest-carbon study permit and SaraCarbon's relationship to Samling.
- [Sarawak Tribune, 9 December 2024](https://www.sarawaktribune.com/sarawak-awards-first-carbon-licence-strengthening-environmental-and-economic-commitments/) confirms the first Sarawak forest carbon licence and names the Marudi Forest Conservation and Restoration Project.
- [SaraCarbon current project page](https://saracarbon.com/marudi/) confirms the 25,675-hectare conservation and restoration scope, management role, forest types, and intended credit generation.
- The forecast `1.45 million tonnes CO2e per year` is not a monetary value.
- [World Rainforest Movement, 23 April 2025](https://www.wrm.org.uy/bulletin-articles/marudi-indigenous-communities-oppose-logging-company-carbon-project-in-sarawak-malaysia) reports Indigenous and NCR-rights opposition. This conflicts with the company's community-position wording. The tracker introduction does not claim that this issue is resolved.

### Special Needs Community Centre

- [DayakDaily, 5 June 2026](https://dayakdaily.com/sarawak-to-build-rm30-mln-special-needs-community-centre-to-deliver-lifelong-care-training-support/) confirms the current RM30 million first phase, Samarahan site, integrated service scope, tender and award targets, construction duration, and May 2028 completion target.
- [Borneo Post, 5 June 2026](https://www.theborneopost.com/2026/06/05/sarawaks-first-special-needs-community-centre-set-for-completion-by-2028/) independently confirms the Phase 1 value, facilities, role in disability support, tender schedule, and completion target.
- The current Phase 1 evidence supersedes the older RM55.7 million plan and January 2026 operations claim for displayed facts. It is not blended with the current card.
- The July 2026 tender target remains open because no accepted source confirms that the tender event occurred.

### One-Stop Early Intervention Centre (OSEIC) Miri

- [UKAS, 30 April 2026](https://ukas.sarawak.gov.my/web/subpage/news_view/39533) confirms the Tudan site, progress assessment, under-seven service scope, RM3.047 million 2024 grant, and RM2.696 million 2025 grant.
- [Borneo Post, 1 May 2026](https://www.theborneopost.com/2026/05/01/oseic-miri-homeless-transit-centres-on-track-for-2026-rollout/) supports the 2026 opening target.
- The earlier October 2024 opening target is rejected. The card does not state whether this is Sarawak's second or third centre because the accepted sources conflict on the ordinal.

### Sarawak Science Centre (SSCiEX)

- [Official SSCiEX site](https://ssciex.org.my/) confirms the current five-storey facility, 22 galleries, more than 400 exhibits, 29 July 2026 launch, and 30 July visitor opening.
- [Official development timeline](https://ssciex.org.my/home/project-development-timeline/) confirms the completed current building state.
- [Sarawak Tribune, 21 July 2023](https://www.sarawaktribune.com/piling-works-on-rm239-mln-sarawak-science-centre-begin/) confirms the piling milestone.
- [SRDC, 20 July 2022](https://www.sarawakrdc.org.my/news-events/news/3/sarawak-science-centre) supports the delivery body.
- The current official five-storey scope supersedes the older 10-storey description. The RM239 million figure is not displayed because the live amount is tied to that superseded design account and no newer scope-matched total was verified.

### Sarawak Infectious Disease Centre

- [SIDC official site](https://sidc.org.my/) supports its establishment, research identity, and mission.
- [SIDC campus page](https://sidc.org.my/new-lab) confirms the Samarahan location, One Health purpose, Biosafety Level 3 scope, and three construction packages.
- [DayakDaily, 1 December 2025](https://dayakdaily.com/sarawak-infectious-disease-centre-to-be-completed-by-oct-2026/) confirms 40.19 percent campus construction progress as of October 2025, the October 2026 completion target, and that the research organisation has operated since February 2024.
- The campus remains `In Progress`. The organisation's operations do not make the unfinished campus `Operational`.
- The older RM200 million figure is not displayed because no accepted current webpage ties it to the present campus scope.

### Miri Combined Cycle Gas Turbine Power Plant

- [Miri City Council, 19 October 2023](https://miricouncil.gov.my/web/subpage/news_view/733) confirms PETROS, the Jalan Pantai and Lutong location, and September 2023 earthworks commencement.
- [Sarawak Tribune, 25 April 2026](https://www.sarawaktribune.com/miri-combined-cycle-gas-turbine-project-reaches-45-completion/) confirms RM2 billion, 500MW, 45 percent completion as of March 2026, PETROS's delivery role, and the end-2027 completion target.
- Only three distinct lifecycle stages are published. No procurement, award, commissioning, or operation milestone was invented to increase the count.

## Inclusion-gate result

All seven additions pass the new-project inclusion gate:

- each is a distinct tracked unit rather than an aspiration, event, adjacent project, or umbrella collision;
- PCDS inclusion is established by the earlier authoritative source review, while current card facts use live public pages;
- identity, location, scope, lead treatment, status, value type, milestones, and source roles are recorded above;
- each card has at least one live project-specific webpage and an evidence-supported outcome to track;
- consequential status claims have an official or project-owner source, or two independent reputable reports;
- English and Bahasa Melayu facts use the same scope, qualifiers, milestone order, dates, and completion flags.
