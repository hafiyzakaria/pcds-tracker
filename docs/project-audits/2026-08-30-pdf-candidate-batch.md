The AIP splits the swine programme into site-level action steps, which strengthens the case against one initiative card and creates the case for a site card.

The AIP identifies MANRED as project owner and DVS as implementing agency, records RM6.5 million for 233 hectares of NCR land compensation, and names anchor farms and a PFA operator that current reporting has since replaced. Treat the AIP parties as historical.
