## Community Social Support Centre Network

### Accepted scope and fields

- Canonical card: `Community Social Support Centre (CSSC) Network`
- Category and status: Social Services, `In Progress`
- Lead: Ministry of Women, Childhood and Community Wellbeing Development / Social Development Council
- Value: `Not disclosed`. The RM40 million PCDS programme figure remains PDF-only and is not displayed.
- Tracked unit: the statewide CSSC rollout, anchored by the operating Kuching centre and planned divisional centres.

### Accepted evidence

- [DayakDaily, 10 February 2022](https://dayakdaily.com/sarawaks-first-community-social-support-centre-to-open-in-kuching-offers-assistance-to-vulnerable-communities/) confirms the agreement for the first Kuching centre, ministry role, one-stop service scope and nine-centre plan.
- [Borneo Post, 8 November 2025](https://www.theborneopost.com/2025/11/08/community-social-support-centre-launched-in-sibu/) confirms that CSSC Sibu was launched on 7 November at Jalan Awang Ramli Amit and that its key was handed to the community operator.
- [UKAS, 17 March 2026](https://ukas.sarawak.gov.my/web/subpage/news_view/36445) records Kuching as operating by that date, records 653 social cases and 760 programmes, and reports April and May 2026 operating targets for Sibu and Bintulu.
- [JKR Sarawak, 2 July 2026 quotation listing](https://jkr.sarawak.gov.my/web/subpage/webpage_view/465) confirms a one-month furniture-supply quotation for TTG and CSSC Bintulu, called on 2 July and closed on 16 July.

The April Sibu operations target is replaced by the stronger achieved event: the November 2025 centre launch and operator handover. The May Bintulu operations target is removed because the July furniture procurement shows that fit-out activity continued after that date. Bintulu operations and additional divisions remain undated open outcomes.

The reader-facing milestone uses the concise wording `Kuching centre operational`. It records the operating status evidenced on 17 March 2026 and does not claim that operations began on that date.

## Sago BioCNG Plant and Gas Distribution Network

### Accepted scope and fields

- Canonical card: `Sago BioCNG Plant and Gas Distribution Network`
- Public display name: `Sago BioCNG and Gas Distribution Project`
- Category and status: Renewable Energy, `In Progress`
- Lead: CRAUN Research Sdn Bhd
- Value: `Not disclosed`. No project-specific public monetary amount survived the value-exhaustion pass.
- Tracked unit: the Kampung Tabo and Kampung Teh pilot combining sago-waste treatment, BioCNG development and local gas distribution.

### Accepted evidence

- [Utusan Sarawak, 12 July 2020](https://utusansarawak.com.my/kampung-tabo-teh-pengguna-biogas-rumbia-pertama-di-dunia/) confirms March 2020 state approval, CRAUN's role and the 23 November 2019 pilot-plant launch.
- [Sarawak Tribune, 19 October 2020](https://www.sarawaktribune.com/strategic-partnership-to-develop-biogas-from-sago-waste/) confirms the development partnership and that the earlier wastewater-treatment plant became operational in May 2019.
- [The Star, 25 May 2023](https://www.thestar.com.my/business/business-news/2023/05/25/craun-makes-breakthrough-in-green-energy) confirms CRAUN's BioCNG plant development and an earlier 147-household scope.
- [TVS, 27 March 2026](https://www.tvsarawak.my/2026/03/27/krisis-tenaga-dunia-sisa-sagu-jadi-emas-hijau/) confirms pipeline installation, the newer scope of about 122 homes and public facilities, and an end-2026 pilot completion target.

The newer 122-home and public-facility scope supersedes the older 147-household figure. Commercial expansion remains conditional on the pilot's success and is not presented as an achieved milestone.

## Implementation decisions

- English and Bahasa Melayu entries use matching scope, status, value treatment and milestone order.
- Both public descriptions are short project introductions rather than timeline summaries.
- Update history records the March 2026 network report, November 2025 Sibu launch and July 2026 Bintulu furniture procurement.
- None of the other 13 reviewed candidates was added or changed.
